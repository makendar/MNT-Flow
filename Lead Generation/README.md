# Lead Generation — demo kit (MNT Flow)

Presentation-ready sales & outreach materials for **MNT Flow**. All companies, people, emails, and phone numbers are **fictional demo data** unless you replace them with approved production content.

## Use in the app

Sign in as a **Sales** or **Admin** demo role → sidebar **Lead Generation**.

- Preview, **copy**, **edit**, and locally save templates
- Three **visual brochures** (Executive, Problem→Solution, WhatsApp mobile)
- **5-touch** cold email and WhatsApp sequences
- Discovery **call script**, **demo checklist**, and **call notes**
- **Demo leads** with pipeline statuses (browser-local persistence)

Nothing is sent from the app — copy into your approved email / WhatsApp tools.

## Folder layout (source-of-truth mirrors)

| Folder | Contents |
|--------|----------|
| `Brochures/` | Pointers to in-app visual brochures |
| `Cold Emails/` | Five-touch sequence (markdown exports) |
| `WhatsApp Templates/` | Five-message sequence |
| `Call Scripts/` | Discovery demo script & checklist |
| `Follow Ups/` | Post-demo and nurture templates |
| `Demo Assets/` | Speaker notes & screenshot placeholders |
| `Client Documents/` | FAQ, forms, proposal placeholders (legal review where marked) |

Canonical editable content lives in `web/src/lib/lead-generation/assets/` and renders in the UI.
